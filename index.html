What the app does

Credify Merchant Insights reads your connected sales data and shows it on one Analytics page.

  • Unified dashboard

    Sales, orders, and trends from every connected store sit on one Analytics page.

  • Filter by store

    Keep all stores in view, or limit the same page to Amazon or one other shop.

  • Channel comparison

    See which connected store the revenue is coming from.

  • Custom reports

    Create a performance report from the sales data you connect.

  • Real-time insights

    Read current numbers instead of exporting files and merging them by hand.

  • Next step

    A short note on the Analytics page suggests what to do with the numbers.

Built for more than one store

Merchants who sell on more than one platform can compare channels without building a spreadsheet.

  • Sign in once with your Credify account.
  • Connect each shop from the same dashboard.
  • Open Analytics and read the stores together.
